How this works

Paper trading only. This app does not connect to your Kalshi account and cannot place a real order.

It automatically discovers the active KXBTC15M contract, reads Kalshi YES/NO prices once per second, tracks the target and countdown, and rolls into the next 15-minute window.

The model combines the Kalshi contract midpoint with BTC's distance from target, seconds remaining, short-term BTC momentum and recent volatility. It looks for a gap between the market price and its estimated probability before taking a simulated YES or NO position.

*The BTC reference feed is used only as a trading signal. Kalshi settlement is determined by its specified CF Benchmarks reference methodology, not this app's BTC reference feed.
