About TerraSignal
This is not a guaranteed earthquake predictor. Exact earthquake time, location and magnitude cannot currently be predicted reliably. TerraSignal identifies unusual recent seismic patterns. True early warning starts after rupture begins and may warn locations before stronger shaking arrives.

On iPhone, open this HTTPS page in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then allow Location when you use the map.
