How the bot works

Paper trading only. This version cannot place a real order or access your exchange balance.

The signal engine combines EMA trend, RSI, MACD, Bollinger position, momentum and volume. Position size is limited by your configured risk per trade and maximum account allocation. Stops use ATR volatility, and the profit target is based on a reward-to-risk multiple.

The bot evaluates completed candles rather than reacting to every price tick. That reduces false entries from short-term noise.

Important: this is a simulation and strategy-testing tool, not a promise of profit. Crypto can move quickly and model performance can change materially.
